Q58Question

A buyer offers $375,000 if the seller signs a general warranty deed. The seller counters with a special warranty deed. This counteroffer is best described as:

  1. a.A binding contract
  2. b.A qualified or conditional acceptance
  3. c.A terminated agreement between all parties
  4. d.Binding once sent to the buyer
Tap the card to flip · swipe for next
Q58Answer
b
A qualified or conditional acceptance

Changing a term (the deed type) means the seller hasn't accepted. A qualified or conditional acceptance is a counteroffer, and nothing is binding until the buyer accepts it.

Offer: general warranty deed
↓
Seller: special warranty deed
↓
Counteroffer; awaits buyer
